A stretched canvas worked from late September to November 2026, partly under its plastic wrapper. It began as a funnel of brown, green, violet and yellow forms in the upper half; through October the funnel was carried down to a point at the bottom, the wrapper came off and grey washes, black lines and red, violet and pink marks spread out across the whole canvas.
