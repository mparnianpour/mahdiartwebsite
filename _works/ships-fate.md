This painting starts from the cruelty of the sea: its merciless waves, and a
season in which the fate of a ship, and of the lives it carries, is put to
the test.

It was painted on unstretched canvas, worked flat on the studio floor. Broad
fields of grey and pink came first, then lines drawn in black and blue, then
washes of violet and grey along the bottom. The last additions came in green
and gold.
