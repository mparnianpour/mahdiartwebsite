An unstretched canvas worked over a month, from 31 August to 29 September 2026. It began as fields of blue, teal and black above a broad violet ground; that night red, black and white figures were drawn into the middle, then silver and black line work, a standing figure at the right, and finally brown, grey and green bands that close the composition along the bottom and sides.
