Three kraft-paper panels painted in one afternoon, hung side by side. Red, pink, cream, yellow and black forms with long pointed shapes went down first; half an hour later they were filled out and joined across the panels, and by evening the panels were laid out on the floor. (The last photo is turned to match the first two.)
