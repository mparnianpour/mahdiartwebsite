A canvas worked on over one day, 30 March 2026, and signed and dated "2026 march" at the lower right. It began as soft pale blue and green washes in overlapping rounded patches, with a patch of strong blue in the middle and a few green, grey and purple strokes. Through the day thick dark green, black and teal lines were drawn over the middle and then across the lower half, and in the evening thin orange lines were added and built up into dense orange, ochre and dark brown brushwork across the top.
