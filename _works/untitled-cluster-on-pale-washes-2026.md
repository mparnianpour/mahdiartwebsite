A cluster of forms on a canvas washed in pale grey, blue and ochre. On 11 November it was a loose arrangement of white shapes with yellow, teal, pink and violet lines; by the next day the shapes were filled with red, brown, yellow, violet and green, and dark blue and violet were added that afternoon.
