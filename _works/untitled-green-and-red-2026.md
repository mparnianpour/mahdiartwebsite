A canvas photographed on 24 November 2026. A pale green wash with vertical drips covers the top, and a red wash with thicker red strokes fills the bottom. Between them, thin teal lines and small circles drawn across the upper middle and right sit above sets of purple and pale blue horizontal lines, wavy purple lines at the right, and pink, orange and red blocks. In the centre, grey, beige and brown-grey shapes are outlined with thick black marks, and a red stroke stands near the right edge.
