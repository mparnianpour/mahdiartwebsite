An unstretched canvas worked on in November 2026. The first photograph, on 14 November, shows a dark grey rounded form in the lower middle beneath an orange wash and a cluster of lilac, magenta and purple shapes, with a narrow column of marks down the right side. A digital image was projected over the canvas on 16 November. By 19 November green lines, small white marks and teal shapes had been added among the forms, and by 21 November crimson and red strokes, a red triangle and smaller coloured marks had been added across the grey form.
