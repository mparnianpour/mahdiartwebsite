An unstretched canvas worked from late August to mid-September 2026. It began as black and grey ink lines on the bare canvas; the next day grey washes and heavy grey bands filled it out into a dense lattice of upright and looping forms. In September thick white, green and blue paint came into the middle, then maroon and black, and finally red, orange and pink.
