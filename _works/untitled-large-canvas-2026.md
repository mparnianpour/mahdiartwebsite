A large unstretched canvas hung on a pink studio wall, photographed on 1 August 2026. Heavy blue and navy lines trace a tall standing form through the middle, with red, pink and rose lines around it, olive green shapes at the top and sides, and pale blue, grey and cream washes behind. Broad dark brown and grey-brown brush marks fill the lower third.
