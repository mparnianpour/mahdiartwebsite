An unstretched sheet photographed on 9 and 10 August 2026. It begins as washes of pale green, grey and brown and streaked blue blocks under a loose white line drawing. By the next afternoon the drawing has become a dense web of black, rust-red and mustard lines. There are later photographs of this work that are not shown here.
