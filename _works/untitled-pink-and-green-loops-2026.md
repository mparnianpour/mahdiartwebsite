An unstretched sheet worked on in early August 2026. On 2 August a looping cluster of green, yellow, teal and plum forms sits on a pink and mauve ground, with the lower part of the sheet still white. By the afternoon of 3 August the sheet has been turned a quarter turn and the white part is painted with a blue-grey ground, a yellow form with green and grey shapes, and a dark red block. There are further photographs from these two days that are not shown here.
