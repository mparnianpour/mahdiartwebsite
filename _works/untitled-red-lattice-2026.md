An unstretched canvas begun on 3 October 2026 as a lattice of thin red lines, drawn with a straight edge and left to drip. The next day thick red shapes were painted into the upper part; on 5 October black, olive, green and yellow forms filled the right half, and later in the month violet, grey and white were added at the edges.
