A stretched canvas from September 2026. It began by taking paint off the canvas with a cloth. By the night of 7 September a pink loop across the top, a pink oval, and orange and brown lines had been added; the next evening teal lines and spray covered the lower half and pale blue lines ran through the middle, and four days later white and dark blue shapes were painted over the spray at the bottom.
