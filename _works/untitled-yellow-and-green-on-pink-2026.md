An unstretched canvas washed in pale pink and yellow, with a brown-black block at the top left, worked on through October 2026. It began on the studio floor as a cluster of grey loops and white shapes; two days later it hung on the wall with the cluster filled in yellow, green and blue, and by the end of the month red and lilac figures had been added at the upper left.
