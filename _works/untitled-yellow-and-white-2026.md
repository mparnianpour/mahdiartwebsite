Opaque yellow and white paint on a stretched canvas, worked on over one day, 15 November 2026, under the light of a projector. A digital image was projected onto the canvas, first in yellow and later in pink, and the work was so heavily influenced by the projected colour that the virtual and the pigment could no longer be told apart.

It began as a dense cluster of yellow and ochre strokes with thick white paint laid over it, on an otherwise bare white canvas. By evening the bare edges had been washed in yellow and ochre, and the white strokes across the cluster had become bolder.
