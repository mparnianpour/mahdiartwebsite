A stretched canvas, worked on over two days, 15 and 16 November 2026, beginning in opaque yellow and white paint. On the first day a digital image was projected onto the canvas, first in yellow and later in pink, and the work was so heavily influenced by the projected colour that the virtual and the pigment could no longer be told apart.

It began as a dense cluster of yellow and ochre strokes with thick white paint laid over it, on an otherwise bare white canvas. By evening the bare edges had been washed in yellow and ochre, and the white strokes across the cluster had become bolder.

The next day magenta, rose, burgundy and dark green marks were added through the middle of the cluster, with a pale pink-brown streaked band along the bottom. Later that day a grid of dark teal dots was added across the top, with dark teal bands down parts of the left and right edges.
