An unstretched canvas worked on between 2 and 21 November 2026. It began with black lines and blue, grey and green forms over a yellow ground; on 10 November pink, violet and thick white paint were worked through it, then dark violet and magenta, and by 12 November it hung on the wall. By 21 November a dark red stroke had been added at the right, a red-brown shape at the lower left, and small red marks near the lower right.
