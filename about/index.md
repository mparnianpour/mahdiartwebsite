## Statement

I paint abstractly, but I don't start from a style or a color scheme. A
painting usually begins with something I can't yet put into words, and the
work is the process of finding a form for it.

That process is slow and visible. I sketch first, often many times. On the
canvas, areas are built up, scraped back and painted over; decisions are made
and undone. What remains at the end is the result of that whole sequence, not
a surface designed to fit a room.

This is why I show the stages. Each painting on this site appears together
with its sketches and the photographs I take as it changes, and the journal
records what happens in the studio day by day. I would like the work to be
looked at the way it was made: slowly, and with attention to how it got there.

## Biography

Mahdi Parnianpour is a painter working mainly in abstraction. Parnianpour's practice
moves between daily drawing and long-developing paintings, and the process
behind each work is documented openly on this site and on Instagram.
