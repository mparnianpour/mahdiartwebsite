- The 2025 heroes are uncropped: floor, walls and neighbouring works show

  batch should add crops to the latest stage of each.

  ones that have titles; the slug can stay.
- Works now lists 3 titled works from 2026 and 17 from 2025. That is fine at
  this size; past about 40 works the page will need year navigation.
- Medium and size are missing for all new works; the pages hide those lines
  until they are set.
