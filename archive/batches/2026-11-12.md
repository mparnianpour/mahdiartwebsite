# 2026-11-12: two works sent in chat

Mahdi sent photos of two works in chat, with their sizes, and asked for each to be posted as one work.
No titles were given, so both have working titles for him to rename.

- [Untitled (Yellow ground)](https://mparnianpour.github.io/mahdiartwebsite/works/untitled-yellow-ground-2026/),
  110 × 80 cm: four stages from 2 to 12 November 2026. A fifth photo (12 November, 16:18) shows the same
  state as the 13:48 one, closer and cut off at the left, so it was left out.
- [Untitled (Cluster on pale washes)](https://mparnianpour.github.io/mahdiartwebsite/works/untitled-cluster-on-pale-washes-2026/),
  80 × 70 cm: three stages from 11 to 12 November 2026. The first photo shows a rose wash along the bottom
  that is not in view in the later photos.

Stages are ordered by the photos' capture times and cropped to the canvas. The photos are not in Drive.
