# 2026-11-15: Untitled (Yellow and white)

Mahdi sent eight photos of one work in chat and asked for it to be put on the site, presented professionally.
He described it as a mix of opaque work in which the colour concept moved and the white brushstrokes became
bolder; no title or size was given.

- [Untitled (Yellow and white)](https://mparnianpour.github.io/mahdiartwebsite/works/untitled-yellow-and-white-2026/),
  100 × 70 cm, painted on 15 November 2026. The size is read from the "100X70" stretcher label visible in
  the photos (the label also says "acrylic and oils", which says what the canvas is for, not what was used,
  so no medium is set). The title is a working title.
- Four stages: the first yellow state (16:01), a yellow projection (16:55), a pink projection (21:25) and the
  final state (21:36). Comparing the 16:01 and 21:36 photos after aligning them shows the changes the last note
  describes: yellow and ochre washes over the bare left edge, lower left and bottom, and many more thick
  white strokes over the cluster.
- Left out, with the same paint state as a stage: a grey-green projection at 15:58 (790 px wide), the canvas in
  a café (17:34) and in a hallway (19:34), and a wider view of the final state (21:26).
- Mahdi then explained "the concept of colour moved": he was so heavily influenced by the projector's projected
  colour that he could not tell the virtual from the pigment. The page text and the two projection notes now say
  so. Still open: whether "a mix of opaque work" means opaque paint, or a mix of opaque and thin layers.

## Later: Untitled (Teal and pink loop)

Mahdi sent eight images of Untitled (Teal and pink loop) again and said it is the same work, whose beginning
technique was taking paint off with a cloth.

- The first stage note and the opening of the page text now state the technique as he gave it. The words
  "washes" and "dripped" are gone from both, since the first layer was made by taking paint off.
- **Correction.** The published stage 2 note said the pink loop, pink oval, orange and brown lines arrived with
  the teal on 8 September. A photo from the night of 7 September (22:55), left out the first time as a mirrored
  selfie, already shows them and no teal. Aligned with stage 1 and stage 2 (flipped back, 662 matched points),
  stage 2 adds the teal lines and spray across the lower half and right side, and pale blue lines through the
  middle. The stage 2 label and note and the page text are corrected. The state in between has no stage of its
  own yet.
- Of the eight images, three were stages already on the page. Held for Mahdi: the 7 September mirrored selfie
  (sharp, but he is in front of the left of the canvas) and the 8 September 17:41 street photo (the canvas is
  only about 380 x 540 px). Skipped: an Instagram story screenshot and two undated collages.
